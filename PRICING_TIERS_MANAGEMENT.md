## Executive Summary

OberaConnect offers three comprehensive pricing tiers designed to meet the needs of organizations at different stages of compliance maturity. All plans include a 14-day free trial, SSL security, daily backups, and 99.9% uptime guarantee.

---

## Tier 1: Starter Plan

**Monthly Investment:** $499/month

**Target Audience:** Small teams getting started with compliance

### Core Features
- **Control Management:** Up to 100 controls
- **Framework Support:** 2 framework mappings
- **Workflow Integration:** Basic workflow integration
- **Support Level:** Email support
- **Compliance Features:**
  - Audit trail logging
  - Standard reporting
- **Team Size:** 5 team members

### Best For
- Small businesses (5-25 employees)
- Organizations new to compliance frameworks
- Teams establishing initial GRC processes
- Limited budget requirements

---

## Tier 2: Professional Plan ⭐ MOST POPULAR

**Monthly Investment:** $1,499/month

**Target Audience:** Growing organizations with complex compliance needs

### Core Features
- **Control Management:** Unlimited controls
- **Framework Support:** All framework mappings
- **Workflow Integration:** Advanced workflow automation
- **Support Level:** Priority support + dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM)
- **Advanced Capabilities:**
  - Custom schema builder
  - Advanced analytics and reporting
  - API access for integrations
  - Custom integrations
- **Team Size:** Unlimited team members

### Best For
- Mid-size organizations (25-500 employees)
- Multi-framework compliance requirements (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, etc.)
- Organizations requiring automation
- Teams with integration needs
- Companies scaling their compliance programs

### ROI Highlights
- Automation reduces manual compliance tasks by 60-80%
- Custom integrations eliminate duplicate data entry
- Advanced analytics provide actionable insights
- Unlimited team members support organizational growth
