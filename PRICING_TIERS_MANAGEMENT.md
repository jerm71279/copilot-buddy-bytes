# Lovable Platform - Pricing Estimate for Management

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** October 2025  
**Prepared For:** Management Review  
**Platform:** Lovable (AI-Powered Development Platform)

---

## Executive Summary

Lovable offers a comprehensive AI-powered development platform with flexible pricing tiers designed to scale from individual developers to large enterprise teams. This document outlines the three main subscription tiers (Free, Pro, Team) plus Enterprise options with detailed cost breakdowns.

**Key Pricing Components:**
- **Subscription Cost:** Based on number of seats/users
- **Cloud Usage:** Usage-based hosting costs (with $25/month free tier)
- **AI Usage:** Usage-based AI feature costs (with $1/month free tier)

---

## Pricing Tiers Overview

### Tier 1: Free Plan

**Monthly Cost:** $0/month

**Target Audience:** Individual developers, hobbyists, and those testing the platform

#### Subscription Features
- **Cost:** $0 per seat
- **Seats:** Unlimited free seats
- **Best For:** 
  - Personal projects
  - Learning and testing
  - Small prototypes
  - Portfolio websites

#### Usage Limits
- **Cloud Free Tier:** $25/month (temporary offering through end of 2025)
- **AI Free Tier:** $1/month (temporary offering through end of 2025)
- **Important:** Must upgrade to add funds beyond free tier limits

#### Example Use Case
**Personal Blog/Portfolio:**
- 500 visits/month
- Blog posts with comments
- Image hosting
- **Estimated Cloud Cost:** $1/month (covered by free tier)
- **Estimated AI Cost:** $1/month (AI-generated summaries/titles, covered by free tier)
- **Total Monthly Cost:** $0

## Tier 2: Pro Plan

**Monthly Cost:** $20/seat + usage

**Target Audience:** Professional developers and consultants

### Subscription Features
- **Base Cost:** $20 per seat/month
- **Enhanced Features:**
  - Priority build queue
  - Extended build times
  - Advanced collaboration tools
  - Enhanced support

### Cost Breakdown (Example: 3 Seats)

| Component | Calculation | Monthly Cost |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| Subscription | 3 seats × $20 | $60.00 |
| Cloud Usage | After $25 free tier | Variable |
| AI Usage | After $1 free tier | Variable |
| **Total** | Base + Usage | **$60+** |

### Example Use Cases

**Small Business Website:**
- 5,000 visits/month
- Product listings, forms, images
- Simple chat assistant (6,500 AI calls/month)
- **Cloud Cost:** $5/month (covered by free tier)
- **AI Cost:** $2/month
- **Usage Cost:** $1/month (after free tiers)
- **Total Monthly:** $61/month (1 seat)

**Team Project Manager:**
- 20 active users
- 10,000 visits/month
- Tasks, file uploads
- AI task suggestions (6,000 calls/month)
- **Cloud Cost:** $15/month (covered by free tier)
- **AI Cost:** $5/month
- **Usage Cost:** $4/month (after free tiers)
- **Total Monthly:** $64/month (1 seat) or $124/month (3 seats)

## Usage-Based Pricing Details

### Cloud Hosting Costs
- **Free Tier:** $25/month (temporary through end of 2025)
- **Pricing:** Pay-as-you-go based on actual usage
- **Includes:** Database, storage, edge functions, bandwidth
- **Typical Costs:**
  - Personal Blog: $1-5/month
  - Small Business: $5-15/month
  - Team Project: $15-30/month
  - E-commerce: $40-100/month

### AI Feature Costs
- **Free Tier:** $1/month (temporary through end of 2025)
- **Pricing:** Pay-as-you-go based on AI calls
- **Includes:** AI-powered features across the platform
- **Typical Costs:**
  - Light Usage (2,500 calls): $1-2/month
  - Moderate Usage (6,500 calls): $2-5/month
  - Heavy Usage (20,000 calls): $10-20/month

## Recommendations

### For Management Consideration:

1. **Start with Pro Plan** ($20/seat) for initial team of 3-5 developers
   - Total estimated cost: $50-100/month
   - Scale up as usage grows

2. **Move to Team Plan** ($40/seat) when team reaches 5+ members or requires:
   - Advanced collaboration features
   - Team workspaces and permissions
   - Enhanced analytics

3. **Consider Enterprise** for organizations with:
   - 10+ developers
   - SLA requirements
   - Custom integration needs
   - Dedicated support requirements
