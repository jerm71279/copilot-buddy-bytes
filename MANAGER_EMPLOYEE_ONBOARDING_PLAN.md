# Manager/Department Head - Employee Onboarding Support Plan

**Version:** 1.0  
**Audience:** People Managers, Team Leads, Department Heads  
**Timeline:** First 30 days of employee start  
**Purpose:** Guide managers through their role in successful employee security onboarding

---

## Manager Responsibilities Overview

As a manager, you play a critical role in:
- Welcoming new team members
- Setting expectations and goals
- Providing context and mentorship
- Ensuring security compliance
- Monitoring early performance
- Addressing onboarding issues

---

## Before Start Date (Week Prior)

### Task 1: Prepare for New Team Member

**Time Allocation:** 2 hours  
**When:** 1 week before start date

#### Administrative Preparation

- [ ] **Review New Hire Information**
  - Read resume and interview notes
  - Review job description
  - Understand background and skills
  - Note any special requirements

- [ ] **Workspace Preparation**
  - Ensure desk/workspace is ready
  - Verify equipment is assigned (coordinate with IT)
  - Prepare welcome materials
  - Add name plate/signage (if applicable)

- [ ] **Team Communication**
  - Announce new hire to team
  - Share start date and role
  - Explain how new hire fits into team
  - Request team support during onboarding

**Email Template to Team:**
```
Subject: Welcome [New Employee Name] - Joining [Date]

Team,

I'm excited to announce that [Name] will be joining our team as [Job Title] starting [Date].

[Name] comes to us with [brief background]. They will be focusing on [primary responsibilities] and will report to me.

I'd like to ask for your help in making [Name] feel welcome:
- Please introduce yourself on their first day
- Be available for questions
- Include them in team activities
- Share any tips about our team culture

Let's give [Name] a warm welcome!

[Manager Name]
```

#### Technical Preparation

- [ ] **Coordinate with IT**
  - Verify account will be ready
  - Confirm equipment assignment
  - Request any special software/tools
  - Ensure application access will be granted

- [ ] **Coordinate with HR**
  - Confirm start date and time
  - Verify benefits enrollment schedule
  - Confirm training schedule
  - Obtain copy of employee onboarding checklist

## First Day

### Morning: Welcome and Orientation

**Time Allocation:** Full morning  
**Your Involvement:** 2-3 hours total

#### Task 1: Greet Employee on Arrival (15-30 min)

**Welcome Checklist:**
- [ ] Be present when employee arrives
- [ ] Warm, enthusiastic greeting
- [ ] Show employee to their workspace
- [ ] Provide welcome packet
- [ ] Explain first day schedule
- [ ] Answer immediate questions
- [ ] Walk employee to HR for initial paperwork

**What to Say:**
```
"Welcome! We're so glad you're here. Let me show you to your workspace and give you an overview of today's plan. 

You'll start with HR for some paperwork, then IT will get you set up with your computer and accounts. After that, I'll introduce you to the team and we'll have some time to talk about your role and what to expect.

I want you to know that it's normal to feel overwhelmed on Day 1 - there's a lot of information coming at you. Don't worry about remembering everything. Take notes, ask questions, and know that we're all here to help you succeed."
```

#### Task 2: Office/Virtual Tour (15-30 min)

**Points of Interest:**
- Team area/desks
- Conference rooms
- Break room/kitchen
- Restrooms
- Emergency exits
- IT support desk
- HR office
- Your office/desk

**For Remote Employees:**
- Virtual workspace overview
- Communication tools tour
- How to find team members
- Virtual office hours
- Remote work best practices

#### Task 3: Team Introductions (30 min)

**Introduction Protocol:**
- Gather team together (or schedule brief 1-on-1s)
- Introduce new employee with context
- Have each team member share:
  - Name and role
  - How they work with this position
  - One fun fact or hobby
  - Best way to reach them

**Your Introduction:**
```
"Team, I'd like to officially introduce [Name], our new [Title].

[Name] will be working on [primary responsibilities] and will be a key part of [projects/initiatives].

[Name], let me introduce you to the team..."

[After intros] 

"I want everyone to remember what it feels like to be new. Please be patient, welcoming, and don't hesitate to offer help. [Name], don't hesitate to ask questions - there are no stupid questions, and we all remember being new."
```

### Afternoon: Role Clarity and Goal Setting

**Time Allocation:** Afternoon  
**Your Involvement:** 2-3 hours

#### Task 4: 1-on-1 Role Overview (60 min)

**Agenda:**

**1. Welcome and Ice Breaker (10 min)**
- Get to know each other
- Share your management style
- Discuss communication preferences
- Set expectations for 1-on-1s

**2. Role Overview (20 min)**
- Review job description
- Clarify responsibilities
- Explain how role fits in big picture
- Discuss success metrics
- Share performance expectations

**3. Team Dynamics (10 min)**
- Explain team structure
- Describe working relationships
- Share team culture and norms
- Discuss collaboration expectations

**4. First Week Goals (15 min)**
- Outline learning objectives
- Set achievable first-week tasks
- Identify quick wins
- Establish checkpoints

**5. Questions and Next Steps (5 min)**
- Answer questions
- Confirm afternoon schedule
- Set next 1-on-1 time

**Key Messages to Convey:**
- "Your success is my success"
- "I'm here to support you"
- "Ask questions whenever you need to"
- "It's okay to make mistakes while learning"
- "We hired you for your skills and judgment"

#### Task 5: End of Day Check-in (15 min)

**Check-in Questions:**
- How was your first day?
- Do you have everything you need?
- Any surprises or concerns?
- What questions do you have?
- How are you feeling?

**Provide Encouragement:**
```
"You did great today. I know it's a lot to take in. Tomorrow will feel a bit more familiar, and it gets easier from there.

Tonight, just relax. Don't feel like you need to study or prepare anything. Come in tomorrow ready to learn more.

If anything comes up tonight or you think of questions, feel free to send me an email and we'll address it tomorrow.

See you in the morning!"
```

## First Week (Days 2-5)

### Daily Manager Activities

**Time Allocation:** 30-60 minutes per day

#### Daily Check-ins (15 min each morning)

**Morning Standup Questions:**
- How are you settling in?
- What's on your agenda today?
- Any blockers or questions?
- What support do you need?

**Purpose:**
- Maintain open communication
- Catch issues early
- Provide guidance
- Build relationship

#### Access and Security Oversight

**Your Security Responsibilities:**

- [ ] **Verify System Access**
  - Confirm employee can log in
  - Check application access is correct
  - Verify department dashboard access
  - Test key tools are available

- [ ] **Approve Role-Based Access**
  - Review assigned role (User/Manager/Admin)
  - Confirm it matches job requirements
  - Approve any special access requests
  - Document business justification

- [ ] **Monitor Security Training**
  - Ensure employee attends security training
  - Verify training completion
  - Reinforce security best practices
  - Answer security-related questions

**Security Approval Form (if required):**
```
Employee: _______
Requested Access: _______
Business Justification: _______
Approval: _______ (Manager Signature)
Date: _______
```

#### Progressive Task Assignment

**Day 2-3: Guided Learning**
- Assign simple, low-risk tasks
- Provide detailed instructions
- Be available for questions
- Review work together

**Day 4-5: Supervised Independence**
- Assign slightly more complex tasks
- Provide general guidance
- Check in periodically
- Give constructive feedback

**Example First Week Tasks:**
- Review team documentation
- Shadow experienced team member
- Complete training modules
- Attend team meetings (observer)
- Small, defined project task
- Practice using key tools

## 30-Day Review Meeting

**Time Allocation:** 60 minutes  
**When:** End of first month

### Meeting Structure

**1. Employee Self-Assessment (15 min)**

Ask employee to reflect on:
- What have you accomplished?
- What are you most proud of?
- What has been challenging?
- What do you need to learn next?
- How do you feel about your role?
- How do you feel about the team?

**2. Manager Feedback (20 min)**

Provide feedback on:
- **Strengths demonstrated**
  - Technical skills
  - Soft skills
  - Cultural fit
  - Initiative and engagement

- **Areas for development**
  - Knowledge gaps to address
  - Skills to build
  - Behaviors to adjust
  - Support needed

**3. Goal Setting for Next 60 Days (15 min)**

Set SMART goals:
- **Specific**: Clear and well-defined
- **Measurable**: Quantifiable outcomes
- **Achievable**: Realistic given resources
- **Relevant**: Aligned with role and team goals
- **Time-bound**: Deadline of 60 days

**Example Goals:**
- Complete [X project] by [date]
- Develop proficiency in [tool/skill]
- Build relationship with [stakeholder]
- Take ownership of [responsibility]

**4. Career Development Discussion (10 min)**
- Discuss long-term career aspirations
- Identify growth opportunities
- Plan skill development
- Discuss potential projects

## Manager Best Practices

### Communication

**Be Accessible**
- Keep calendar updated
- Respond to messages promptly
- Hold regular 1-on-1s
- Create safe space for questions

**Be Clear**
- Provide explicit expectations
- Give specific feedback
- Clarify priorities
- Explain context and rationale

**Be Supportive**
- Offer encouragement
- Celebrate wins
- Help problem-solve
- Advocate for your team member

### Security Leadership

**Model Security Behavior**
- Follow all security policies yourself
- Use strong passwords and MFA
- Handle data appropriately
- Report security concerns

**Reinforce Training**
- Reference security concepts in regular conversations
- Praise security-conscious behavior
- Correct security lapses kindly
- Make security part of team culture

**Approve Thoughtfully**
- Question excessive access requests
- Apply principle of least privilege
- Document business justifications
- Review access regularly

### Feedback

**Provide Regular Feedback**
- Don't wait for formal reviews
- Give feedback in real-time
- Balance positive and constructive
- Be specific with examples

**Receive Feedback**
- Ask how you can better support them
- Listen openly to concerns
- Act on feedback when appropriate
- Model continuous improvement

## Common Challenges and Solutions

### Challenge 1: Employee Seems Overwhelmed

**Signs:**
- Withdrawn or quiet
- Excessive note-taking
- Reluctant to ask questions
- Staying late every day

**Solutions:**
- Reassure that it's normal
- Slow down the pace
- Break tasks into smaller pieces
- Check in more frequently
- Share your own onboarding experience

### Challenge 2: Employee Can't Access Systems

**Signs:**
- Repeated login failures
- Missing applications
- Can't see department data
- Error messages

**Solutions:**
- Immediately escalate to IT
- Document specific error messages
- Stay with employee until resolved
- Follow up after resolution
- Ensure no repeat issues

### Challenge 3: Employee Not Completing Security Training

**Signs:**
- Training marked incomplete
- Missing from training sessions
- Hasn't mentioned training

**Solutions:**
- Check training schedule with HR
- Ask directly about training status
- Emphasize importance and priority
- Schedule protected time for training
- Follow up on completion

### Challenge 4: Cultural Fit Concerns

**Signs:**
- Doesn't engage with team
- Misses team events
- Communication style mismatch
- Seems isolated

**Solutions:**
- Have 1-on-1 conversation
- Understand their perspective
- Explain team culture expectations
- Facilitate team bonding
- Give it time (30 days is early)
- Provide specific, actionable feedback
